Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Hermes Fitness is a private application for personal use by its owner.

Data Access

Hermes Fitness may access health, activity, sleep, recovery and related data from Oura only after explicit authorization by the account owner.

Purpose

The data is used solely for the owner's personal fitness, training, recovery and wellness tracking and analysis.

Data Storage

Data is stored in a private self-hosted environment controlled by the application owner.

Data Sharing

Personal data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or provided to third parties for commercial purposes.

Authorization

Access to Oura data requires OAuth authorization and can be revoked by the owner at any time.

Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy can be sent to the contact email associated with the Hermes Fitness Oura application.
